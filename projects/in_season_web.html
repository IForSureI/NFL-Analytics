2026 In-Season Snippets

Twelve quick charts on the season in progress, the kind a staff looks at on a Monday. Every point opens its breakdown: a quarterback's efficiency by down and by throw depth, a back's success by down, a receiver's role and value by depth, a team's offense and defense split into run and pass, a pass rusher's week by week, a kicker's makes by distance. The small bars in each card are week by week.

Hover any player or team for the breakdown. On a phone, tap.

Method

  • Data: nflverse play-by-play (EPA, CPOE, xpass, xYAC from the nflfastR models) and nflverse team colors. Regular season, completed weeks only: a week counts once all of its games are in.
  • Dropbacks include sacks and scrambles; two-point tries and penalty no-plays are left out. Success = the play gains positive EPA. CPOE is in percentage points.
  • Early-down PROE: 1st and 2nd down, win probability 20-80%, outside the last two minutes of each half. Pressure proxy = sack or QB hit (play-by-play has no hurries). Receiver shares are over the games each player appeared in.
  • Field goals: expected make rate from a logistic model on distance, distance squared and a dome flag, fit on 2022-25 regular-season kicks. Blocked kicks count as misses.
  • Every headline number on this page is checked against the published charts' findings.json when the page is built. Code: projects/in_season_web.py. Static charts and write-up: reports/2026-in-season-snippets.
